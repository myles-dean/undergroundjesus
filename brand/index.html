03 / Logo use

Let the
mark breathe.

Good work needs room. Give the logo a clear field around it—at least the height of the “U” on all sides—and choose the highest-contrast color pairing.

Use this
UJUNDERGROUND
JESUS

Clear contrast, generous space, no competing message.

Avoid this
UNDERGROUND
JESUS

Do not stretch, add effects, recolor freely, or crowd the mark.
